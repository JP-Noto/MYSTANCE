# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

- **CONTRIBUTING**: the contribution regime is now declared upfront. Ideas, counter-examples and
  critiques arrive as issues and are integrated under the author's pen, credited; text accepted
  through a pull request carries the contributor's grant to integrate, modify and license it with
  the whole, including under the written agreements the non-commercial clause reserves; the
  contributor stays credited and free to reuse their own text elsewhere. Purpose: the corpus keeps
  a single rights holder, so the licensing lever survives its first accepted contribution.

- **ASSISTANT DE RÉFÉRENCE** : the prohibition on exposing the architecture now states its scope. It
  covers the end-user experience; it does not govern operating surfaces (workshop views, system maps,
  dashboards of whoever runs the system), which belong to deployment. An operating surface shown to an
  end user becomes an end-user experience, and the prohibition applies in full. Two attached points:
  what an operating surface does not show to a third party is **excluded**, not hidden; and the setting
  that decides what is disclosed stays separate from the assistance level, which decides what is
  offered. Prior art credited: least privilege and psychological acceptability (Saltzer & Schroeder,
  1975), and established permissions-UX practice, which keeps permissions in admin settings and
  preferences in the user's own preference centre.
